# Paper Evidence and Interpretation Robustness

P1.4-D begins only after a canonical topology has been accepted.

## Canonical evidence

Generate:

- topology summary;
- membership table;
- landmark table;
- component and overlap summaries;
- topology-axis ball profiles;
- optional post-freeze colour profiles;
- point-indexed local-colour evidence;
- candidate fixed-layout maps;
- artifact provenance.

## Point-indexed local colour

For an observation belonging to several balls, the default local-colour
statistic is the equal-weight mean of the colouring means of all containing
balls.

The statistic allows substantive patterns to be compared across landmark
orders without matching arbitrary ball numbers.

It is descriptive and is not a causal or structural parameter.

## Radius robustness

Alternative radii are declared only after the canonical radius is approved.
They are sensitivity values, not candidates allowed to replace the approved
radius automatically.

## Landmark-order robustness

Across repeated orders, report observation-level and aggregate stability of
point-indexed local colours.

High correlation supports stability of the broad pattern; it does not imply
exact observation-level invariance.

## Handover boundary

P1.4-D produces analytical evidence. A paper conversation decides substantive
meaning, literature framing, captions and journal presentation.
